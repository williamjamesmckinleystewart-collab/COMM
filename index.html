Where the money can go

Once you post, the payment sits in the contract. From there it has exactly two ways out:

  • To the tutor, when the student confirms the session happened.
  • Back to the student, if they cancel before anyone accepts.

Nobody else can move it. Not the tutor, not another student, and not whoever built this page.

One catch: after a tutor accepts, the request can't be cancelled. If the session never happens, the payment stays locked.
